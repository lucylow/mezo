## 2. The Full-Stack Bitcoin Economy Vision

Mezo's ecosystem strategy is built on a singular, ambitious thesis: Bitcoin should be productive capital, not idle digital gold. The protocol creates a closed-loop economic system where every participant—BTC holders, MEZO lockers, borrowers, and liquidity providers—is incentivized to contribute to the network's growth.

The economic flywheel operates as follows:
1. **BTC deposited** as collateral → MUSD stablecoin minted
2. **MUSD deployed** across DeFi, spending, or yield strategies
3. **Fees generated** from borrowing, swapping, and bridging
4. **Fees distributed** to veBTC and veMEZO holders
5. **MEZO emissions** incentivize continued participation
6. **veMEZO locked** to boost yields and direct emissions

This circular economy ensures that value accrues to long-term participants rather than short-term speculators. The protocol's official documentation emphasizes that "Mezo's BTC-native DeFi protocol introduces a ve mechanism" that aligns incentives across the entire ecosystem, creating a sustainable foundation for long-term growth.


## 3. The ve(3,3) Coordination Layer: Core of Ecosystem Efficiency

At the heart of Mezo's strategy is the **ve(3,3) tokenomic model**, a battle-tested mechanism adapted from successful protocols like Curve (veCRV) and Aerodrome (veAERO). This dual-token system coordinates the two primary stakeholder groups in the ecosystem.

### 3.1 Dual-Token Architecture

| Token | How Obtained | Primary Function |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **veBTC** | Lock BTC | Vote on gauges to direct emissions; earn BTC-denominated fees |
| **veMEZO** | Lock MEZO | Boost veBTC voting power up to 5x; earn rebase rewards and gauge incentives |

The veMEZO token "cannot direct emissions, claim fees, or govern the protocol on its own"—it exists purely as a booster asset that amplifies the productivity of veBTC positions. However, veMEZO is "productive on its own" because holders "can direct their votes to any veBTC gauge and collect the incentives that BTC holders post".

### 3.2 The 5x Boost Multiplier

The boost mechanism is the primary value driver for veMEZO. When veBTC and veMEZO are paired, they create a combined "boosted weight" that determines the user's share of fees and influence over emissions. This creates a powerful incentive for users to lock MEZO for extended periods (up to 4 years) to maximize their boost multiplier.

### 3.3 Epoch-Based Governance

Mezo operates on a **7-day epoch cycle** starting every Thursday at 00:00 UTC. Votes must be cast each epoch, and rewards are distributed based on voting activity during that period. This weekly cadence creates a recurring, time-sensitive opportunity for yield optimization—precisely the friction point that our Auto-Compounder Vault addresses.

### 3.4 Why ve(3,3) Succeeds on Mezo

The ve(3,3) model has been proven to create sustainable, long-term aligned ecosystems. Aerodrome, which Mezo has partnered with as its primary liquidity hub, achieved TVL exceeding **$1 billion** on Base using this exact mechanism. Mezo's implementation applies the same "habit loop" to Bitcoin lending: "Lock, vote weekly, direct emissions, earn fees, and optimize over time". By building on this proven foundation, Mezo reduces execution risk and accelerates ecosystem maturity.

### 4.3 Emissions and Incentive Structure

MEZO emissions are designed with Bitcoin-halving-inspired mechanics to sustain long-term growth. The protocol distributes rewards across multiple channels:
- **veMEZO rebase rewards**: Automatically distributed to veMEZO holders each epoch
- **Gauge incentives**: Posted by veBTC holders to attract veMEZO votes
- **Liquidity mining**: Incentives for MUSD and MEZO liquidity providers

This multi-channel emission strategy ensures that MEZO tokens flow to the most productive participants, creating a meritocratic system that rewards active contribution to the ecosystem.
