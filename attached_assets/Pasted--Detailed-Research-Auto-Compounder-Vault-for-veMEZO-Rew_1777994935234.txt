As articulated in the official announcement by Vaish Puri, "Bitcoin has spent a decade proving it can hold value. It hasn't yet proven it can do much else." Mezo Earn introduces a **Bitcoin‑native incentive framework** where locked BTC earns BTC‑denominated fees, directs value flows, and participates in a permissionless marketplace for influence. Central to this framework is the dual‑token coordination mechanism comprising **veBTC** and **veMEZO**, which together enable a dynamic, capital‑efficient economy built on Bitcoin.

The **Auto‑Compounder Vault for veMEZO Rewards** is designed to operate natively within this Mezo Earn ecosystem. Its sole purpose is to optimize the utility and yield of **veMEZO**—the coordination asset that amplifies Bitcoin's governance weight and captures value from the matching market. This research document provides an exhaustive analysis of the vault's alignment with Mezo Earn, drawing directly from the official article "Introducing: Mezo Earn" and supplementary protocol documentation. The findings confirm that the Auto‑Compounder Vault is not merely a peripheral DeFi application but an **essential infrastructure component** that solves critical coordination frictions inherent in the ve(3,3) matching market described by Mezo.


## 2. The Architecture of Mezo Earn: A Primer

### 2.1. Core Design Principles

The Mezo Earn framework is built upon three explicit requirements for a sustainable Bitcoin‑native DeFi ecosystem:

1. **Attract and retain deep liquidity** in BTC and stablecoin pairs.
2. **Support a competitive stablecoin savings rate** (for MUSD).
3. **Ensure sustainability through native fees**, reducing reliance on inflationary emissions over time.

To achieve these goals, Mezo employs **explicit coordination** using the **MEZO token** as the network's "coordination asset." The article states: *"MEZO is the instrument that routes incentives, adjusts parameters, shapes liquidity, and captures the execution value generated by the chain."*

### 2.2. The Curve Wars Precedent

The design of Mezo Earn draws direct inspiration from the **Curve Wars**. The article notes: *"Curve never intended to create a public incentive battleground, yet its voting and emissions model made influence something users were willing to compete for. Convex emerged, vote‑escrow positions turned into strategic assets, and suddenly, the value of liquidity incentives became a market with an observable price."*

The critical takeaway is that *"capital coordinates when you give it levers that matter."* Mezo Earn applies this lesson to Bitcoin, creating a **permissionless marketplace for influence** where BTC provides the base governance weight and MEZO supplies **bounded amplification** that must be earned or acquired through market competition.

### 2.3. The Dual‑Token Coordination System

The Mezo Earn framework revolves around two vote‑escrowed tokens:

| Token | Underlying Asset | Max Lock | Primary Function | Yield Source |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **veBTC** | BTC | 28 days | Vote on gauge allocations; direct value flows | BTC‑denominated chain fees (swaps, borrowing, bridging) |
| **veMEZO** | MEZO | 4 years | Amplify veBTC voting power (up to 5x); no independent voting rights | Rebase rewards + incentives from veBTC gauges |

The article emphasizes: *"Governance power cannot drift away from Bitcoin... Bitcoin retains the sovereignty."* This design ensures that while MEZO coordinates incentives, **Bitcoin remains the governance anchor**.

### 2.4. Boosted Weight and Capital Efficiency

The combined voting weight of veBTC and veMEZO results in a **Boosted Weight**. The article explains: *"The boost depends on a user’s relative share of the total veBTC and veMEZO supply. The boost scales with relative exposure. Larger BTC positions require correspondingly greater MEZO exposure to reach the maximum multiplier."*

Crucially, *"because boost is bounded, MEZO never overrides Bitcoin as the governance anchor; it only changes how effective a BTC position is in the economic game of directing value."* This bounded amplification mechanism ensures that **MEZO optimizes the capital efficiency** of a Bitcoin position without diluting Bitcoin's foundational role.


## 3. The Matching Market: veMEZO as an Influence Commodity

### 3.1. Permissionless Marketplace for Influence

The most innovative aspect of Mezo Earn is its **matching market** between BTC holders and MEZO holders. The article describes it as *"not a side‑effect of holding two assets at the same time, but instead an explicit market between BTC and MEZO."*

When a user locks BTC, the system creates an **individual veBTC gauge** for that position. veMEZO holders may then choose which of these gauges to support with their voting weight. The amount of veMEZO directed to a gauge becomes the **boost factor** for that specific BTC position.

### 3.2. Demand and Supply Dynamics

The market operates on clear demand and supply forces:

**Demand Side (BTC‑heavy participants):**
- *"A BTC‑heavy participant wants a higher yield multiplier."*
- *"They post incentives on their personal veBTC gauge."*

**Supply Side (MEZO‑heavy participants):**
- *"A MEZO‑heavy participant wants to maximize their incentive earnings."*
- *"They scan the market for the highest‑paying gauges and direct their votes to capture the best possible return."*

The article further clarifies: *"BTC holders do not need MEZO to participate in Mezo Earn at the base level... but MEZO enables them to amplify their impact. MEZO holders, in turn, do not need BTC to earn from the system; they can direct votes toward veBTC gauges that pay attractive incentives."*

### 3.3. The Coordination Friction

This matching market, while elegant, introduces significant **coordination friction** for individual participants:

1. **Continuous Monitoring Required**: veMEZO holders must constantly scan the market to identify which veBTC gauges are offering the most attractive incentives.
2. **Weekly Voting Obligations**: *"Each epoch, veBTC voters determine how the network’s broader fee and reward flows should be allocated."* Votes do not persist across epochs, requiring weekly action.
3. **Decaying Voting Weight**: veMEZO weight decays linearly over the 4‑year lock period, requiring active management to maintain boost effectiveness.
4. **Gas Costs**: Each interaction (claiming rebases, claiming incentives, re‑voting) incurs BTC gas fees, which may be prohibitive for smaller positions.

The article implicitly acknowledges this complexity by stating that *"the value of liquidity incentives became a market with an observable price."* In such markets, aggregation and automation become essential for maximizing returns.


## 4. The Auto‑Compounder Vault: Solving veMEZO Coordination Friction

### 4.1. Core Value Proposition

The **Auto‑Compounder Vault for veMEZO Rewards** directly addresses the coordination challenges inherent in Mezo Earn's matching market. Its value proposition is threefold:

1. **Automated Reward Harvesting**: The vault's keeper bot claims both **rebase rewards** (MEZO emissions distributed to all veMEZO holders) and **gauge incentives** (payments from veBTC holders seeking boost) on behalf of all depositors.
2. **Strategic Vote Allocation**: By aggregating veMEZO from multiple users, the vault achieves economies of scale in vote optimization. It can direct aggregated voting weight to the highest‑yielding veBTC gauges, capturing superior returns compared to individual, uncoordinated voting.
3. **Compounding Reinvestment**: Claimed rewards (in MEZO) are automatically re‑locked into the veMEZO position, extending the lock to the maximum 4‑year duration to maintain full voting weight and maximize future earning potential.

### 4.2. Direct Alignment with Mezo Earn Mechanics

The vault's operations map directly to the Mezo Earn framework described in the article:

| Mezo Earn Component | Auto‑Compounder Vault Integration |
|:---|:---|
| **veMEZO** | Vault accepts veMEZO NFT deposits; issues liquid vveMEZO shares |
| **Rebase Rewards** | Keeper claims weekly rebase emissions for all deposited NFTs |
| **Gauge Incentives** | Keeper claims incentives from veBTC gauges that the vault's votes support |
| **Boosted Weight** | Aggregated veMEZO enables optimal boost ratios for all depositors |
| **Matching Market** | Vault acts as a professional "supply‑side" participant, optimizing incentive capture |

### 4.3. Solving the "Red Queen" Dynamic

The article notes that *"larger BTC holders receive less incremental benefit from small amounts of MEZO, forcing them to either acquire more MEZO or pay incentives over multiple epochs to maintain a high multiplier."* This creates a **Red Queen dynamic** where individual veMEZO holders must constantly increase their position size or optimize their vote allocation just to maintain relative standing.

The Auto‑Compounder Vault mitigates this dynamic through **collective scale**:
- By aggregating many smaller veMEZO positions, the vault achieves a total weight that can compete with larger individual holders.
- The vault's automated vote optimization ensures that depositors' collective weight is directed to the most lucrative gauges, maximizing incentive capture.
- Gas costs are socialized across all depositors, making participation economically viable for holders of any size.

### 4.4. The "Convex for Mezo" Thesis

The article explicitly invokes the **Convex Finance** precedent: *"Convex emerged, vote‑escrow positions turned into strategic assets, and suddenly, the value of liquidity incentives became a market with an observable price."*

The Auto‑Compounder Vault is positioned as **Convex for Mezo's veMEZO**. Just as Convex aggregated veCRV to optimize Curve gauge voting and capture incentive value, the vault aggregates veMEZO to optimize Mezo Earn's matching market. The business model is identical: charge a performance fee (e.g., 10%) on the enhanced yield generated through aggregation and automation.

This parallel is not coincidental—it is a direct application of a battle‑tested DeFi primitive to a new ecosystem. The article's own framing validates this approach: *"Mezo Earn takes that lesson and applies it to Bitcoin."*

## 6. Technical Integration with Mezo Earn Components

### 6.1. Interaction with veMEZO Contract

The vault interacts directly with the **veMEZO NFT contract** to:
- Accept deposits of veMEZO NFTs (`safeTransferFrom`)
- Claim rebase rewards (`claimRebase`)
- Increase locked amounts with compounded rewards (`increaseAmount`)
- Extend lock duration to maximum (`increaseUnlockTime`)

These interactions are non‑custodial: users retain ultimate ownership of their veMEZO NFTs and can withdraw at any time by burning their vault shares.

### 6.2. Interaction with Gauge Controller

The vault interacts with the **Gauge Controller** to:
- Claim incentive rewards from veBTC gauges that the vault supports (`claimRewards`)
- (Future enhancement) Allocate aggregated veMEZO voting weight to optimal gauges (`vote`)

The keeper bot's optimization logic evaluates pending rewards across all active gauges and directs votes to maximize incentive capture for depositors.

### 6.3. MUSD Fee Collection

The vault satisfies the hackathon's mandatory MUSD integration requirement by collecting performance fees in Mezo's native stablecoin. After claiming rewards, the keeper bot:
1. Swaps the fee portion (10% of gross rewards) to MUSD via Mezo's DEX.
2. Transfers the MUSD to the vault's treasury address.
3. (Optional) Deposits treasury MUSD into the MUSD Savings Vault for additional yield.

This integration creates a direct link between the vault's revenue model and the MUSD economy described in the article: *"MUSD is Mezo’s bitcoin‑backed stablecoin."*

### 6.4. Epoch Timing and Keeper Scheduling

The keeper bot is scheduled to run shortly after each epoch ends (Thursdays at 00:05 UTC). This timing ensures that all rewards accrued during the previous epoch are claimable and that votes can be re‑allocated for the new epoch. The bot includes profitability checks (`checkUpkeep`) to ensure that compounding only occurs when pending rewards exceed estimated gas costs plus a 10% profit margin.
